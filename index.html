How to Use:

  1. Select a frame from the options above.
  2. Click the "Upload Image" button to select your profile picture (must be 1:1 aspect ratio).
  3. Use pinch gestures to zoom in/out on mobile devices.
  4. Drag the image to reposition it within the frame.
  5. Click the "Share Image" button to share your framed profile picture.
  6. Use the shared image as your new profile picture!
